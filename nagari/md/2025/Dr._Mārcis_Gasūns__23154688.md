Dr. Mārcis Gasūns. Computational linguist and Sanskrit scholar with over 20 
years of experience across lexicography, philology, NLP, and digital 
corpora. Author and editor of 14 printed books on Sanskrit (3834 pages 
total), including dictionaries, readers, and textbooks in Russian. Founder

languages and modern technology. Founded the Sanskrit-Russian Parallel 
Corpus with semi-automated morphological markup.
