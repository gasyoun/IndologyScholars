## 7. knbrao — 2025-02-09 09:10:33

Мне только одно непонятно, почему нельзя указать главы в калькуттском 
издании? Дейссен указывает. 

It's because this Calc. ed. had continuous verse numbering all through the 
parva, though the pushpikā places do have the adhyāya numbers; we may take 
that it is the ideosyncratic order in this edition. And these were always 
cited from it as such, in the [parva, sloka] manner!

To indicate the adhyāya numbers in it for the verses, requires doing the 
donkey work to browse through all those pages and make a list; who has such 
a patience and necessity?

Mostly people go by the easy ways, unless it is a do-or-die condition, or 
having "a crazy head"!!
