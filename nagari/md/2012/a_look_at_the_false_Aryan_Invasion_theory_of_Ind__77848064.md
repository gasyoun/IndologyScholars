fact that logic does not attract many minds.

Aryan Invasion theory has time and again fallen as a junk theory created by

the scrupulously negatively oriented minds of the colonial masters of India

when they were astonished to see that Hindus in ancient India and even at

the time of the invasion of the Europeans were far ahead of them in

Medicine, literature, linguistics, science, technology, philosophy,

mathematics -- in short every aspect of human development was far more

advanced than the "European Masters". They needed to convince themselves

that they were in fact the masters and not the savages that a weak moment

in history of India had put them in such master's position. Well, recent

victory of Viswanathan Anand in world chess championship at least proves

that Indians are inferior to none and they could be masters in the mind

game that their forefathers have created



India was philosophically united and Bharat - the India from the times of

yore was even a bigger nation with greater bonds between the people of vast

nationalities living in different regions of it but sharing almost common

ethnic food, habits, cultural similarities, etc. If you take the non-tribal

population of Kerala then you find that all castes and religions in Kerala

were having similar genetic structures, hence when Ezhavas, Christians,

Nairs, Muslims and Brahmins are analysed genetically they have common gene

pool.



Hence their genetic similarity disproves many assumptions made based on

illogical conclusions that Brahmins were the Aryan race invading Kerala.

While Brahmins form 1 % of the population of Kerala but the fair/white

population of Kerala is not limited to them only and constitutes about 25 %

of the population of the state.Also Brahmins and other castes in Kerala and

rest of India have fair share of White and black people in their numbers.

The genetic make of these castes now suggests that it was never intruded by

external gene pool but all non-tribal castes are similar in nature although

the castes may be different. The "Aryan Invasion" theory (AIT) does not

have any logical explanation to this.



1. AIT as invented by Max Muller saw hordes of chariot driven invaders

crossing over into India. First of all it is impossible for any charioteers

to steer their chariots over the hindukush mountains and arrive in India in

vast hordes.



2. In Indian history or even mythology never has such instances where there

has been an invasion of external forces like the Aryans intruding into

India been recorded although the Indian historians/philosophers have vast

accounts of their history and mythology.  How was this missed out? Neither

has it happened during the vedic period nor before or after.



3. It has been observed that any migrant population always keeps track of

its mother nation - mother culture. Were the Aryans so ignorant of their

culture that they failed to even record their forefathers/mother nations

nostalgic memory. Never in one of the Vedic literature, shastras,

scriptures and manuscripts were even a feeble mention of the fact that the

Aryans migrated to some nation and from where they came from. The

experiences and surroundings mentioned in the Vedas evidently are from the

Indian subcontinent only. Why any semblance of European country side,

weather, flora and fauna is absent from vedic literature?



4. Vedic culture was the first culture which first proposed inequality

which gave birth to the caste system and saw humans divided into classes.

It recognised inequality of person and created the concept of godhood. It

is in this context that the first divisions in society happened - there

were those who belonged to this very same society who didn't believe in its

in-equal set up and moved away from it. These were the dasas. Max muller

interprets them as Dravidians - which is basically a wrong interpretation

of the whole vedic literature. Vedas segregates dasas as non-believers in

vedic system and Aryans as believers in vedas. Hence Aryans were the noble

people who irrespective of their gene pool believers in Vedic culture while

the same race people who revolted and moved away from this culture were

de-recognised by the vedic people as dasas. While in the earlier vedic

times there were 3 vedas and all of them speak of this gradual division of

the social order the fourth is already seen to have formed after the

division was totally set in. Thus different nationalities which accepted

vedic culture were called Aryans and those who didn't adhere to it were

un-aryan. In Mahabharat - Krishna calls - Jarasandha - unaryan - although

he is from Bihar which is supposed to be the Aryan heartland (today). But

Krishna is actually addressing the un-noble behaviour of Jarasandha who is

a villain. Krishna himself is Black in colour but is an Aryan, similarly he

addresses several kingdoms from south of India as Aryan Kingdoms - which

Max Muller had termed dravidian heart land. Swami Vivekananda had said in

his times after travelling all over India  that he can't find much of a

difference in the people of India except for some regions (these areas were

more influenced by mixture of central asian and mongoloid, turkish, blood

rather than any thing from Europe). Swamiji was himself proud of the fact

that he had more of mongoloid and tatar blood than any of the "European -

so called Aryan" blood in his veins.



IN wake of so many evidences and facts the AIT falls as junk theory and

can't pass of as a serious effort. In modern day genetical study and as

more scientific facts and figures develop such tyrannical false hood will

fall away.

Harish
