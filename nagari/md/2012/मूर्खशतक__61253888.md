Ref: Report on the search for Sanskrit manuscripts, 1906-1907 to 1910-1911
(1911)
     <http://ia600306.us.archive.org/35/items/reportonsearchfo00shasuoft/>

MM Hara Prasad Shastri has reported a Sanskrit work titled "मूर्खशतक" (vide
p.10 under "Miscellaneous"). He has given the following description of this
work:

मूर्खशतक is a humorous work in 26 verses, which describes 100 different
kinds of mUrkhas or fools. The author thinks that a person who purchases
real property by borrowing money is a fool. A merchant who indulges in
humour is a fool. The man who expects affection from a Kaayastha is a fool.
One who goes to a fortune-teller to seek his fortune is a fool. A man with
a hoarse voice is a fool if he attempts to sing. If a beggar longs for a
hot dish is a fool, and so on.
