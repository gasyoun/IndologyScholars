Welcome to the Sanskrit lexicons prepared since 1994 by the Institute of 
Indology and Tamil Studies, Cologne University. 
The 36 dictionaries are organized primarily by the secondary language 
(English, German, etc.), and then by date of publication (1832 till 1976). 
Each dictionary has several types of display (B L A M), as well as PDF scan 
and XML (in SLP1) files for download (D).
