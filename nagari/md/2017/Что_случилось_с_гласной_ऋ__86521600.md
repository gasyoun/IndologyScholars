## 5. Narayan Prasad — 2017-02-21 04:24:11

Here, the sUtra इको यणचि is applicable. The previous इक् (i.e. इ, उ, ऋ, लृ)
changes to यण् (i.e., य्, व्, र्, ल्). The vowel may be short or long.
So, *ऋ* + *अ* ---> र् + *अ = **र*

<<Но почему это называется гуной: *अ* + *ऋ *= *अर् *>>

It's not the sandhi process "*अ* + *ऋ *= *अर्*" that is guNa.
Actually as per Panini sUtra 1.1.2, each of the varNa अ, ए, ओ is called
guNa.

It will be rather a long reply to clear all your doubts. It's worthwhile at
least reading some commentary on P.1.1.1 to 1.1.5. In Russian I am not
aware of any commentary. But in English and German.
