## 9. Narayan Prasad — 2017-10-22 03:54:54

Namaste !



The main difference lies in the inclusion of the svara (accent) also in the

अष्टाध्यायी of पाणिनि, चान्द्रव्याकरण of चन्द्राचार्य and सरस्वतीकण्ठाभरण

of भोजदेव. The latter two are called the great revised editions of Panini,

the last being the biggest in size consisting of more than 6400 sutras. The

other non-Paninian systems of Sanskrit grammar are smaller in size due to

exclusion of accent part.



The suffixes यु and वु are changed to अन and अक in Panini by the sutra

युवोरनाकौ (पा॰7.1.1).

Then the question arises why to use यु and वु at all ? The answer is: the

inclusion of sometimes two anubandhas for taking into consideration the

accent also make the suffixes longer. For brevity this was done.



For accent, in Panini two different suffixes तृच् and तृन् are there. If

accent is neglected, you don't require two different suffixes, only one

will be enough.



I hope, this much explanation is sufficient to clarify the matter.



Best wishes

Narayan Prasad
