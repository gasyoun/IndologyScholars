      - *Analysis*: "of those situated in the Dandaka forest".

   3.

      "demons").

      - *Analysis*: Indicates possession, "of the rakshasas".

   4. 

   

   *एक (eka)*

   - *Form*: Nominative singular masculine.

      - *Analysis*: "one", subject of the verb.

   5. 

   

   *एव (eva)*

   - *Form*: Particle.

      - *Analysis*: Emphasizes the preceding word, "only".

   6. 

   

   *अकम्पन (akampana)*

   - *Form*: Proper noun, nominative singular masculine.

      - *Analysis*: The name "Akamapana", the subject.

   7. 

   

   *नाम (nāma)*

   - *Form*: Indeclinable.

      - *Analysis*: "by name", used to indicate a name.

   *कथमपि (kathamapi)*

   - *Form*: Indeclinable compound.

      - *Analysis*: "somehow" or "by any means".

   9. 

   

   *जीवन् (jīvan)*

   - *Form*: Present participle, nominative singular masculine.

      - *Analysis*: "living" or "surviving".

   10. 

   

   *दुःखार्त (duḥkhārta)*

   - *Form*: Adjective, nominative singular masculine.

      - *Analysis*: "afflicted by sorrow".

   11. 

   

   *लङ्कां (laṅkāṃ)*

   - *Form*: Accusative singular of लङ्का (laṅkā, meaning "Lanka").

   *कथंचित् (kathaṃcit)*

   *राक्षसाधिराजं (rākṣasādhirājaṃ)*

   - *Form*: Accusative singular compound of राक्षस (rākṣasa, "demon") and 

      अधिराज (adhirāja, "great king").

      - *Analysis*: "the king of the demons".

   15. 

   

   *दशग्रीवं (daśagrīvaṃ)*

   - *Form*: Accusative singular of दशग्रीव (daśagrīva, "ten-headed").

      - *Analysis*: Refers to Ravana, "the ten-headed one".

   16. 

   

   *वैश्रवणं (vaiśravaṇaṃ)*

   - *Form*: Accusative singular of वैश्रवण (vaiśravaṇa, son of Vishrava).

      - *Analysis*: Another name for Ravana.

   17. 

   

   *रावणम् (rāvaṇam)*

   - *Form*: Accusative singular of रावण (rāvaṇa, "Ravana").

      - *Analysis*: Direct object, "to Ravana".

   18. 

   

   *उपगम्य (upagamya)*

   - *Form*: Gerund.

      - *Analysis*: "having approached".

   19. 

   

   *खरदूषणविनाशनं (kharadūṣaṇavināśanaṃ)*

   - *Form*: Accusative singular compound of खर (khara) and दूषण (dūṣaṇa), 

      both names of demons, and विनाशन (vināśana, "destruction").

      - *Analysis*: "the destruction of Khara and Dushana".

   20. 

   

   *निवेदयांचकार (nivedayāñcakāra)*

   - *Form*: Perfect tense, third person singular.

      - *Analysis*: "reported" or "informed".
