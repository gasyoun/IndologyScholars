# New indological website

> 1 сообщений · 1 участников · 2009-06-23 — 2009-06-23

## 1. Alex — 2009-06-23 16:26:28

I have just posted in the web a site named "El Portal de la India
Antigua", dedicated to diffuse the achievements of the ancient indian
civilization in the Spanish speaking world, at the address:
http://www.elportaldelaindia.com/
For now it is only in Spanish but I intend to translate it to English,
and to enlarge it and improve it whenever possible. 
*It provides an extensive number of links to books (about 300) and
articles (150, and more coming) related to ancient India and freely
available in the web, commented briefly in order to orient people in
an increasingly confusing forest of publications. 
*It includes, as well, two articles written by me about cosmogony and
eschatology in the Rigveda accompanied by the translation of several
complete hymns (more will be added in the future).
*Another section intends to be a step by step sanskrit course based on
my own experiences and difficulties with the language.
*All of the above are free. 
*Finally, the portal contains three works of mine that try to
alleviate a little bit the dearth of indological publications in
Spanish.
