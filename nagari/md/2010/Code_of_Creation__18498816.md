## 3. achyut karve — 2010-05-25 19:20:05

The history of the west including that of islam is totally based on written

records.  However language in its naive form evolved much earlier.  The

point that needs to be appreciated is that the building blocks of language

namely alphabets are a product of specific effort.  One needs to appreciate

that the structure of the alphabets in sanskrit as well as in most of the

indian languages is phonetic.   Therefore one needs to differentiate between

languages whose script is phonetic and those whose script is representative.

 All 26 or less letter languages have representative scripts.  Secondly

languages other than indian do not indicate the length of the vowels.  The

main difference between sanskrit and other languages including the ancient

ones is the ability of sanskrit to resolve the consonant from the vowel,

thus giving rise to inumerable compound vowels and consonants.  Lastly,

before the west was born sanskrit had already evolved a grammar.  This goes

on to prove that sanskrit scholars much before B.C. 500 where engaged in

regularizing language, meaning language was a subject of deliberation.
