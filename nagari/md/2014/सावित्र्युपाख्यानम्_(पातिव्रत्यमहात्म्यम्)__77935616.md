> सावित्र्युपाख्यानम् २७७- २८३ वनपर्व
>
> Verse - 3.277.2
>
> *_मूलम्- _**_mūlam-_**__*
>
> द्यूते दुरात्मभिः क्लिष्टाः कृष्णया तारिता वयम् ।
>
> जयद्रथेन च पुनर्वनादपहृता बलात् ॥2॥
>
> dyūte durātmabhiḥ kliṣṭāḥ kṛṣṇayā tāritā vayam |
>
> jayadrathena ca punarvanādapahṛtā balāt ||2||
>
> *_पदविभागः- _**_padavibhāgaḥ-_*
>
> द्यूते दुरात्मभिः क्लिष्टाः कृष्णया तारिताः वयम् । जयद्रथेन च पुनः वनाद् अपहृता बलात् ॥
>
> dyūte durātmabhiḥ kliṣṭāḥ kṛṣṇayā tāritāḥ vayam | jayadrathena ca 
> punaḥ vanād apahṛtā balāt ||
>
> *_अन्वयः- _**_anvayaḥ-_**_
> _*वयम् द्यूते दुरात्मभिः क्लिष्टाः कृष्णया तारिताः । पुनः जयद्रथेन च बलात् वनाद् अपहृता ॥
>
> vayam dyūte durātmabhiḥ kliṣṭāḥ kṛṣṇayā tāritāḥ | punaḥ jayadrathena 
> ca balāt vanād apahṛtā ||
>
> *_प्रतिपदार्थः- _**_pratipadārthaḥ-_**__*
>
> वयम्  =vayam  = we
>
> द्यूते = dyūte = in game of dice
>
> दुरात्मभिः = durātmabhiḥ = by the evil-natured people
>
> क्लिष्टाः = kliṣṭāḥ = afflicted, tormented
>
> कृष्णया = kṛṣṇayā = by draupadī
>
> तारिताः = tāritāḥ = are saved or rescued, escaped from
>
> पुनः = punaḥ = again
>
> जयद्रथेन च = jayadrathena ca = by Jayadratha (husband of duśśalā, sister 
> of duyodhana)
>
> बलात् = balāt = by force
>
> वनाद् = vanād = from forest
>
> अपहृता = apahṛtā =carried away
>
> *_तात्पर्यम्- _**_tātparyam-_**__*
>
> 1. The evil-souled had put us to shame in the game of dice but she, 
> the sister of Krishna, had come there to our rescue; and then again, 
> in this forest, Jayadratha took her away forcefully. [2]
>
> 2. When we were afflicted at the game of the dice by those 
> wicked-souled ones, it was Krishna that delivered us. And she was 
> forcibly carried off from the forest by Jayadratha.
