## 13. narayan prasad — 2014-07-30 12:05:36

In this first scan page of the Bengali edition:

आयात् = प्राप्त हइबेन, i.e. will achieve.



As per the Astadhyayi sutra, "छन्दसि लुङ्-लङ्-लिटः" (पा॰3.4.11) - in vedic

literature, the aorist, imperfect and periphrastic past verb-forms are used

in any tense, i.e. the past form is used also in the present and future.

One can find such verb-forms even in the महाभारत.



--- Narayan Prasad
