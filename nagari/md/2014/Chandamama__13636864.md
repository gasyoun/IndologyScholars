Namaste

This is the link to "चन्दमामा" (=Moon Uncle) an age old 

picture-story-magazine of India, which was run in all different Indian 

languages and Samskrtam as well. 

You can find in archives, all books in the form of scanned images; There 

are some latest ones in Unicode Devanagari as well. Just select Sanskrit 

from the list above. Whole page changes to Samskrtam. 

Here in, the language is easy to follow, and stories are really interesting 

to read. The same are in English as well, so in case of doubt you can just 

read the same issue in English.

It was India's favorite magazine, for children and adults both; 

[chanda=Moon, mAmA=Uncle. :) as he is called when children are introduced 

to him for first time.]

Happy reading stories. Happy Learning Samskitam.

-Regards
