Hello Marcis Gasuns,



  Thank you for your note.



The best way to submit comments, or requests for improvements to Unicode 

documents is through the form on this page:

http://www.unicode.org/reporting.html . Comments made there are tracked, 

and will be reviewed and responded to. It's helpful if you can be very 

specific about what is not correct, and suggest possible fixes.



I'm afraid I don't have any suggestions for dealing with Adobe or Microsoft 

applications.



Best Regards,



  -- Andy Heninger
