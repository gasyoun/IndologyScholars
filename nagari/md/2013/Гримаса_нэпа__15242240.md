## 6. śrīdṛṣṭvā — 2013-03-05 16:08:20

Actually, title translation depends on all topic, how they explain it.
For example, titles of American movies sometimes translated with totally 
another word.
I suggest you to read and translate your article first, when you will 
understand sense of topic, you can name your translation as you would like 
to.
Literal translation can be like "The ugly face of NEP", but much better to 
use some epithet that will represent the sense of article.  Гримаса - it is 
Russian epithet, not a precise, not literal. Maybe you'd like to name it 
"New vision of NEP".
If you unable to create epithets by your imagination, you can give precise 
name of article, like "Сriticism of NEP" or "Review of NEP from Modern 
point of view".
