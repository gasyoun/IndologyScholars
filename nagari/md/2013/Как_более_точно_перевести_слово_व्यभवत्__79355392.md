## 2. Usha Sanka — 2013-12-22 09:52:14

Namaste

ब्रह्म वा इदमग्र आसीदेकमेव तदेकं सन्न व्यभवत्

"In the beginning this (the Kshatriya and other castes) was indeed Brahman/

one only.

Being one, he did not flourish."

-Swami Madhavananda

(may be) - व्यभवत् = वि(शेषः)अभवत्। That's what I make out from the meaning

of the word, (if it helps some way).

---------------------

Reading "Brihad Aranyaka Upanishad," collided with a phrase: ब्रह्म वा

इदमग्र आसीदेकमेव तदेकं सन्न व्यभवत्

Its meaning to me more or less clear, but it is not clear how to translate

the word व्यभवत्

Maybe someone knows, please show me.

विभू- is also origin of विभूति।

may be व्यभवत्- related to that- "getting into/assuming different form(s)."

Please see-

Roth-Bohtlingk - Ignore German and see Sanskrit Examples.

http://www.sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/PWGScan/disp2/index.php

  2) gleichkommen, erreichen, erfüllen; ausreichen, zureichen (vgl. उद्): न

10, 4, 2, 8. fgg. 14, 4, 2, 23. fgg. एकं (एका) वा इ दं वि बभूव
