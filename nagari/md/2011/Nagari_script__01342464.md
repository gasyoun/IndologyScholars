## 3. Serge — 2011-07-21 22:59:12

The first is anunāsika+llo ँल्लो (ṁ)llo as in the sandhi न्+ल्= ँल्ल्
(where the double ल् said to be nasalized). E.g. तस्मिन्+लोके =
तस्मिँल्लोके।

The second is llo+anunāsika ल्लोँ llo(ṁ) - a useless combination that
never occurs.
