Вот начало статьи из Monier-Williams, смотрите 3-е значение:

आर्तव ārtavá (ī) (ṛitu )
1. belonging or conforming to the seasons or periods of time,
seasonable R. Kum. Ragh. Vikr. . ;
2. menstrual, relating to or produced by this discharge Suṡr. ;
3. a section of the year, a combination of several seasons AV. TS.

## 4. narayan prasad — 2011-05-23 02:48:27

The word आर्तव is formed by ऋतु + अण् प्रत्यय. The अण् suffix being णित्,
the initial ऋ of ऋतु gets वृद्धि --> आर्.
