Dear All,

 

Despite my love for all religions and for Christ as well. I have never

civilisations, but God chose those who were susceptible to believe in

his Miracles without proof and these people were chosen again and

again and again. Stupidity repeated again, again and again by none

other God :-).

rational thinking of so many people in this world that millions were

sacrificed for nothing. Europe hated jews and made them pay for the

crimes that the Roman empire had committed on a man called Jesus.

Especially semetic religions thrive on this, and the price we had to

pay for this unquestioned faith has been enormous. 100s of millions of

people were sacrificed for the sake of religion or indirectly due to

them by their followers. Had it been not for religions there would be

no genocides, no holocaust, no hitlers brutality at least, no catholic

inquistions, no witchery and burning of innocent women in the name of

God.

 

God should be some thing personal and should be the fountain of love

within which allows us to see clearly other humans and animals as

equals and nothing else. All else in the name of God is crime against

humanity and a crime against the values developed by the spiritually

developed in the human race.

 

Thanks and regards

 

Harish
