## 2. cape verde — 2011-03-11 16:12:50

Dear Vladimir,

The fact that Indians have lost so much of the traditions of Yoga and others
that it would be more easy to find them in Russia and Germany in their
original sanskrit version that in India.

In India these things are so disorganised and as per the masses so unwanted
that it would be much easier to find some thing american here than India
book and that too from ancient times.

When I was in Russia I could find so many books on Yoga, Indian vedic
traditions, ayurveda as compared to India that after coming back to India I
was unable to find them here.

it is quite a sad story. But let me see if I can find it from my friends who
may know it. But please don't keep any hope that I can be certain to find
it.

It is like searching for a needle in the haystack.

Thanks and regards

Harish

## 6. Vasu Srinivasan — 2011-03-11 21:04:24

uvazhaemii g. Vladimir

MLBD has published a two-volume of Yoga Vasishta fully in Sanskrita. No
notes, no explanations ..just text.

There is also another 4 volume publication with Sanskrita text and English
translation

http://www.mlbd.com/BookDecription.aspx?id=1669

There is also an abridged edition by Swami Venkatesananda - The Supreme Yoga
with Sanskrita sloka and English text.

http://www.mlbd.com/BookDecription.aspx?id=8926

Just a minor correction it is Yoga VaasiShTha (not Yoga VasiShTa)
