After long and difficult deliberations, the local organising committee of 

the WSC2023 has agreed unanimously that the WSC2023 will be held online. 

The International Association of Sanskrit Studies has confirmed our 

decision. In view of the ever-changing COVID situation, we lack sufficient 

certainty to plan for a face-to-face conference in Canberra.
