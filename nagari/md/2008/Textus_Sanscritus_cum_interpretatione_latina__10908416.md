## 1. Duri — 2008-08-11 13:46:22

I have download Bopp's "Nalus Maha-bharati Episodium" from Google
Books. I am thinking of making it into an interlinear version. As to
the Sanskrit text I am using the one from SacredBooks.net.
Unfortunately thakara and dakara have traded places. I have already
mentioned the problem with the webmaster, eventhough it might be my
browser's fault (IE6). The Latin text is harder to find. No good OCR
for Latin seems to be available. Therefore I will have to type the
text myself. I think an interlinear Sanskrit-Latin electronic edition
of Bopp's text would be a valuable learning aid. Interlinear editions
exists, but Latin morphology would allow for a closer word-to-word
correspondence than a modern language. Not to mention the educational
potential of comparing two ancient languages. If the text is already
available, please let me know.
Best regards.

## 2. Rodrigo Diaz — 2008-08-28 16:30:46

The bilingual version (the edition of 1832) is available from Google
Books, too.
URL: http://books.google.com/books?id=zTgOAAAAIAAJ
Bopp, Franz (1791-1867): Nalus : Maha-Bharati episodium / textus
sanscritus cum interpretatione latina et annotationibus criticis
curante Francisco Bopp. - Altera, emendata ed. - Berolini : Nicolai,
1832. - XV, 239 p.

I guess, you have got this one:
URL: http://books.google.com/books?id=1DEVAAAAYAAJ
This is the first fascicle of the second edition containing only the
Sanskrit text (came out 1830). The complete edition is that of 1832.

Bopp's first edition from 1819 is still not freely available from
Google Books or another digital collection, so far I can see.

salve
