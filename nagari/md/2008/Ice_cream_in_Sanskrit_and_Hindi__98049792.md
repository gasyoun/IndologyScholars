## 5. Kiran Paranjape — 2008-08-27 15:36:26

In Sanskrit,
Ice  = him = हिम
cream = shara = शर ( also means arrrow as per the context)
         or saara = सार ( also means essence as per the context)
So either himashara or himasaara as per your liking.

As for Hindi,
Icecream is a very commonly used word in Hindi.
As are many English words incrporated in daily usage Hindi language.

## 7. doc — 2008-08-28 05:33:34

Hello,
I agree that the best strategy is to transliterate and treat ice-cream as a
loan word.  The commonly used word as well as th accepted word in all
dictionaries is ice-cream transliterated in Devanagari. The same is good for
Marathi and Gujarati which have just accepted the word as such and
transcribe it in a similar manner. Words like lamp-shade have received a
similar treatment in Marathi and Gujarati. The only other solution is to
gloss with a long explanation.
                 Neologisms of the type him+saara only make life difficult
for native Hindi users like us who do not know how to resolve the
combination as cream with ice or ice with cream or simply ice-cream.
