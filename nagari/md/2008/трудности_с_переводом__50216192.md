## 11. Dirika — 2008-09-07 15:19:46

а правильно ли так разбить словосочетание
tattadupAdhiliptatvAdatyantanirmalaM на сандхи:
tat + tad + upAdhi + lipta + tvAd + atyanta + nirmalaM
что тогда значит lipta  ?

## 12. narayan prasad — 2008-09-07 16:47:20

No. It should be as follows:

tat + tad + upAdhi + liptatvAd + atyanta + nirmalaM

lipta = plastered, smeared, painted, closely united, intent, clung, embraced
liptatva = abstract noun from lipta, the suffix -tva = -ness, -ity etc.
liptatvAd = (ablative singular form of liptatva) due to/owing to/ because
of liptatva
