Graha - Planets
In Sanskrit the word "graha" means to seize or to grab, take hold of.
Simplistic view is that it means planets so, the planets take hold of
your life. There nine Grahas or planets are called Surya or ravi (the
Sun), Chandra (the Moon), Mangal (Mars), Budha (Mercury), Guru or
Brahpathi (Jupiter), Shukra (Venus), Shani (Saturn), Rahu (Northern
lunar node or eclipse of the sun) and Ketu (Southern lunar node or
the
eclipse of the moon). The Sun is not a planet, it is a star. Rahu and
Ketu are not planets, and indeed do not even represent physical
bodies
The Grahas are divided into two groups, “Saumya” or benefic and
“Krura" or malefic. The Waxing Moon ( the moon moving from new Moon
to
Full Moon), Mercury, Jupiter and Venus are known as benefices. The
Sun, Saturn, Mars, Waning Moon (the moon moving from Full Moon to the
New Moon), Rahu and Ketu are classified as malefic. Graha influence
each other in various ways. The rishis saw a relationship between the
movement of the planets and the ups and downs of persons life. Vedic
Astrology explores these variations in order to help us anticipate
challenges and comprehend their meaning. And thus to help us live our
lives better. The sign in which a planet is located is of great
importance .Two planets in the same sign are conjunct. All planets
have drishti or aspects. Or to put it in simpler terms they "look" at
other planets and signs. All planets aspect the sign or planet placed
exactly opposite to them, i.e., in the 7th house from them. This is
normally called the 7th house aspect, or the full aspect. Apart from
this 7th aspect, some planets have "special" aspects. Mars aspects
the
4th and the 8th houses from its position. Jupiter aspects the 5th and
the 9th houses from its position. Saturn aspects the 3rd and the 10th
houses from its position. Ketu aspect the 7th and Rahu the all houses
from their position The houses and the planets aspected by a planet
get influenced by the planet. The effect will be good or bad
depending
on the nature, condition, lordship etc of the aspecting planet and
also on the relation between the two planets. If the sign is
favourable to the planet, the qualities of the planet are expressed
more strongly and auspiciously.
