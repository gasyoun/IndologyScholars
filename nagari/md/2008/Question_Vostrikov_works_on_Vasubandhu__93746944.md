# Question: Vostrikov works on Vasubandhu

[Читать оригинальный тред в Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/d/msgid/nagari/58faf01e0809101656j7d6263c0h97376c7ab7db01c7@mail.gmail.com)

## 1. Siddhacarya — 2008-09-11 02:56:46

In the "Introductory Note" to  Vostrikov's "Tibetan Historical Literature"
it is stated that:

"In 1934, Vostrikov published the article *Logical Works of Vasubandhu*, and
the next year he completed the monograph *Logic of Vasubandhu* which is a
comprehensive treatment of the problem on the basis of all available
sources."

While in Vostrikov's article "*The Nyayavartika of Uddyotakara and the
Vadanyaya of Dharmakirti*" (*Indian Historical Quarterly* v. XI, No. 1,
March 1935) he states in a footnote (p.5 n.14) that his article "The Logical
Works of Vasubandhu**" is "to appear before long."

Does anybody know if these two works (the article and the monograph) were
ever actually published and if so where?

Thanks.
