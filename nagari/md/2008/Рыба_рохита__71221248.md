Rooee, Ruhoo (Cyprinus rohita): no less celebrated in India than the
Carp in Europe : abounds in the fresh water rivers of the Gangetic
province and those of Ava and Assam; and in the ponds of the former is
propagated with great care, being considered as the most valuable
fish...; its beauty, both in form and color, equals its value for the
table, and of all fresh water fishes it is, by many, considered the
most excellent; length - frequently three feet.
