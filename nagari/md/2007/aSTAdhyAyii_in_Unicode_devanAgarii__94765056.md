## 5. Marcis — 2007-07-18 05:20:30

Dear Narayan,

  There is a simple solution and you can manage that on your own. All
you need is to download three files:
http://groups.google.com/group/Nagari/web/Astadhyayi+Unicode+Transliteration.rar
- it is the transliteration.
http://www.emeditor.com/pub/emed6004esx.msi - it is the soft for
convertation from one encoding to another.
http://nagari.googlegroups.com/web/EmEd_Nagari_convert_macros.rar -
macros, than convert from tranlsiteration to devanagari and vice
versa.
  Copy the text of Panini's sutras to EmEditor and Run the macro
needed. Everything works perfect on any Windows platform. The Macros
were developed by Mihas from Minsk.
  Tarabalu's software's biggest and only problem is that it is like a
brick - nothing can be copied out, nothing can be added by the user.
It should better be just an Excel file, much better. It would become
five times more popular.

  Regards, Marcis
