## 7. narayan prasad — 2007-05-31 06:50:25

Views of M B Emeneau:

 "Otto Boehtlingk (1815-1904) was so much impressed by the Paninian method
of analyzing a language that he wrote the grammar of the Turkic Yakut
language of Siberia , in which the working out of phonology, morphology and
syntax is detailed and complete in a very Panini-like fashion. It was
published under the title <Die Sprache der Jakuten> from St Petersburg in
1851(Ref:M B Emeneau, <India and Lingustics>, JAOS, Vol.75, 1955, p.150, fn
18)."
