I wanted to introduce myself to the honorable forum members and our
Moderator.  Thank you for the invitation which I receive as an honor.
     I am a student of Gitopanishad, which I first studied, as a
source book of Sanskrit, as well as a text of religion and
metaohysics, from Brahmananda Saraswati (Dr. Ramamurti Mishra) ca.
1970.
     Good show, I look forward to receiving from you honored friends.
Does anyone know the Bhagavata Purana much?
     Somewhere in Bahgwat, there is a story of a sage, brought up in
total brahmacarya, and having no concept of a mother.  He thought that
brahmacaris were produced from tree like a mango, or ripened in sun
until they fell.  Then some women came upon the gurukula, and he
believed that they must be boys (no facial hair and high boy voices)
who caried horns (like trumpets maybe? ) on their chests, to explain
thick chests of ladies.
     Can an honored forum member direct me to this incident of the
Bhagwat?
     Best wishes in any case.
