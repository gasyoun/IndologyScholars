  If I'm not wrong Narayan has done quite a lot in this field, but the
question remains. Is there or does anybody knows someone who could and
would be interested in compiling a book, a concordance from let's say
some 10-15 Sanskrit primers and grammars. Only the rules.
  For example - all the passages for internal sandhi. I've seen that
in Pondichery lately there has been published the next volume of a
vyakarana dictionary that quotes examples and is of great interest,
but does not care about anything else than Indian tradition. What I
would propose is Whitney, Buhler, Macdonnell, Thumb, Knauer, Coulson,
Zaliznyak, Renou and several other.
  It would take some 20 years to compile that one by hands, so no good
idea. I guess I'm the only onem interested in it. But for me it's time
to master bhraj, to read Kabirdas, the time has come. I can't compile
the concordance by myself, only editing if needed at all.
