7.भविष्यचिन्तायां वर्तमानः नैव विनाश्यः 

7.bhaviṣyacintāyāṃ vartamānaḥ naiva vināśyaḥ

भविष्यकल्पनायां किं, वर्तमानो विनाश्यताम्?।

वर्तमाने य आनन्दः, किं भविष्ये स लभ्यताम्?॥

bhaviṣyakalpanāyāṃ kiṃ, vartamāno vināśyatām?|

vartamāne ya ānandaḥ, kiṃ bhaviṣye sa labhyatām?||

            कदाचित् रात्रौ प्रतिवेशिनः गृहे कलहकोलाहलम् अशृणवम्। समीपे 
स्थितत्वात् कोलाहलः सुस्पष्टं श्रूयते स्म। पत्नी कथयति स्म- “यदि पुत्री 
भविता, तर्हि तस्याः नाम मनोरमेति भवतु। पुत्रः यदि भविता तर्हि तन्नाम श्याम 
इति भवतु।” पतिः इमे द्वे अपि नाम्नी नेच्छति स्म। सः पुत्र्याः सुशीलेति, 
पुत्रस्य च प्रांशु इति नाम कर्तुं इच्छति स्म। पुत्रः पुत्री वा जन्मापि 
नालभेतां, ततःपूर्वमेव तन्नाम्नि दम्पत्योः कलहः नितरां हास्यास्पदं आसीत्।

            kadācit rātrau prativeśinaḥ gṛhe kalahakolāhalam aśṛṇavam| 
samīpe sthitatvāt kolāhalaḥ suspaṣṭaṃ śrūyate sma| patnī kathayati sma- 
“yadi putrī bhavitā, tarhi tasyāḥ nāma manorameti bhavatu| putraḥ yadi 
bhavitā tarhi tannāma śyāma iti bhavatu|” patiḥ ime dve api nāmnī necchati 
sma| saḥ putryāḥ suśīleti, putrasya ca prāṃśu iti nāma kartuṃ icchati sma| 
putraḥ putrī vā janmāpi nālabhetāṃ, tataḥpūrvameva tannāmni dampatyoḥ 
kalahaḥ nitarāṃ hāsyāspadaṃ āsīt|

            प्रश्नाः

            praśnāḥ

1. पतिपत्न्योः कलहः कदा कुत्र अभवत्?

1. patipatnyoḥ kalahaḥ kadā kutra abhavat?

Where and when did the quarrel between wife and husband happen?

2. पत्नी किं कथयति स्म?

2. patnī kiṃ kathayati sma?

What was the wife saying? 

3. पत्युः कथनं किम् आसीत्?

3. patyuḥ kathanaṃ kim āsīt?

What was the husband’s say?

4. किम् अयं कलहः उपहासकारी न आसीत्?

4. kim ayaṃ kalahaḥ upahāsakārī na āsīt?

Was this quarrel not ridiculous?

5. इयं कथा कां शिक्षां ददाति?

5. iyaṃ kathā kāṃ śikṣāṃ dadāti?

What lesson does this story teach?

 *अनुवादः*

*anuvādaḥ*

*Translation*

            Does the happiness of present get destroyed in the imagination 
of future? Can the happiness of present be obtained in future?

            Once, at night, I heard some quarrelling voices in the 
neighbour’s house. Being very near, the voice was clearly heard. Wife was 
saying- “If we have a daughter, we will name her Manoramā; if a son is 
born, then we shall name him Śyām.” Husband did not like both the names. He 
wanted to name daughter as Suśīlā, and son’s name, Prāṃśu. Neither 
daughter, nor even son was born; the quarrel of husband and wife looked 
ridiculous.
