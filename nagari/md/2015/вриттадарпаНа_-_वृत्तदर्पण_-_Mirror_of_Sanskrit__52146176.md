Dear Friends,



A 15-minute video prepared by me has been available on youtube at 

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CNnUhll0zzA



In it, I have explained at the beginning the basic theory of Sanskrit 

prosody and followed it up with recitations of illustrative verses of 31 

meters, chosen from well-known works of Classical Sanskrit literature.  The 

verses are displayed in DevanAgarI and an English translation follows.  At 

the end are mentioned a few books, available online, for further study and 

reference.  The recitations are as taught in Indian schools.



I hope you enjoy it...
