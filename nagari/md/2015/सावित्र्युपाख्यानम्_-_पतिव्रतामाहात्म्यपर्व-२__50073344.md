Verse - 3.277.40

*मूलम्- **mūlam-*

मान्यानां तत्र वृद्धानां कृत्वा पादाभिवन्दनम् । 

वनानि क्रमशस्तात सर्वाण्येवाभ्यगच्छत ॥40॥

mānyānāṃ tatra vṛddhānāṃ kṛtvā pādābhivandanam । 

vanāni kramaśastāta sarvāṇyevābhyagacchata ॥40॥

*पदविभागः- **padavibhāgaḥ-*

मान्यानां तत्र वृद्धानां कृत्वा पाद-अभिवन्दनम् । वनानि क्रमशः तात सर्वाणि 
एव अभ्यगच्छत ॥

mānyānāṃ tatra vṛddhānāṃ kṛtvā pāda-abhivandanam । vanāni kramaśaḥ tāta 
sarvāṇi eva abhyagacchata ॥

*अन्वयः- **anvayaḥ-*

तात, तत्र मान्यानां वृद्धानां पाद-अभिवन्दनम् कृत्वा क्रमशः वनानि सर्वाणि एव 
अभ्यगच्छत ॥

tāta, tatra mānyānāṃ vṛddhānāṃ pāda-abhivandanam kṛtvā kramaśaḥ vanāni 
sarvāṇi eva abhyagacchata ॥

*प्रतिपदार्थः- **pratipadārthaḥ-*

तात = tāta = O son (addressing some young one with affection)

तत्र = tatra = there (then)

मान्यानां = mānyānāṃ = of the respectable ones

वृद्धानां = vṛddhānāṃ = of the elders, aged/learned ones

पाद-अभिवन्दनम् = pāda-abhivandanam = touching feet (in respect, for seeking 
blessings)

कृत्वा = kṛtvā = having done

क्रमशः = kramaśaḥ = one after the other, in order

वनानि = vanāni = forests

सर्वाणि एव = sarvāṇi eva = all

अभ्यगच्छत = abhyagacchata = visited, (went)

*तात्पर्यम्- **tātparyam-*

1. O Yudhishthira, there offering her respects to the venerable aged 
persons, by touching their feet, from forest to forest she went, journeying 
along. [40]

2. There, son, worshipping the feet of the aged ones, she gradually began to 
roam over all the woods.

Verse - 3.277.41

*मूलम्- **mūlam-*

एवं सर्वेषु तीर्थेषु धनोत्सर्गं नृपात्मजा । 

कुर्वती द्विजमुख्यानां तं तं देशं जगाम ह ॥41॥

evaṃ sarveṣu tīrtheṣu dhanotsargaṃ nṛpātmajā । 

kurvatī dvijamukhyānāṃ taṃ taṃ deśaṃ jagāma ha ॥41॥

*पदविभागः- **padavibhāgaḥ-*

एवं सर्वेषु तीर्थेषु धन-उत्सर्गं नृपात्मजा । कुर्वती द्विज-मुख्यानां तं तं 
देशं जगाम ह ॥

evaṃ sarveṣu tīrtheṣu dhana-utsargaṃ nṛpātmajā । kurvatī dvija-mukhyānāṃ taṃ 
taṃ deśaṃ jagāma ha ॥

*अन्वयः- **anvayaḥ-*

एवं नृपात्मजा सर्वेषु तीर्थेषु धन-उत्सर्गं कुर्वती द्विज-मुख्यानां तं तं 
देशं जगाम ह ॥

evaṃ nṛpātmajā sarveṣu tīrtheṣu dhana-utsargaṃ kurvatī dvija-mukhyānāṃ taṃ 
taṃ deśaṃ jagāma ha ॥

*प्रतिपदार्थः- **pratipadārthaḥ-*

एवं = evaṃ = thus

नृपात्मजा = nṛpātmajā = daughter of the king

सर्वेषु = sarveṣu = everywhere

तीर्थेषु = tīrtheṣu = holy places

धन-उत्सर्गं = dhana-utsargaṃ = donating, giving money (in charity)

कुर्वती = kurvatī = (she) doing

द्विज-मुख्यानां = dvija-mukhyānāṃ = of the important dvijas (the first 3 
varṇas, =twice-born)

तं तं देशं = taṃ taṃ deśaṃ = each place

जगाम ह = jagāma ha = went

*तात्पर्यम्- **tātparyam-*

1. Thus, giving away great wealth in all the places of pilgrimage, did the 
Princess visit several distant lands and kingdoms inhabited by the most 
excellent wise persons. [41]

2. Thus the king’s daughter distributing wealth in all sacred regions, 
ranged the various places belonging to the foremost of the twice-born ones.

Verse - 3.278.2

*मूलम्- **mūlam-*

ततोऽभिगम्य तीर्थानि सर्वाण्येवाश्रमांस्तथा । 

आजगाम पितुर्वेश्म सावित्री सह मन्त्रिभिः ॥2॥

tato’bhigamya tīrthāni sarvāṇyevāśramāṃstathā । 

ājagāma piturveśma sāvitrī saha mantribhiḥ ॥2॥

*पदविभागः- **padavibhāgaḥ-*

ततः अभिगम्य तीर्थानि सर्वाणि एव आश्रमान् तथा । आजगाम पितुः वेश्म सावित्री 
सह मन्त्रिभिः ॥

tataḥ abhigamya tīrthāni sarvāṇi eva āśramān tathā । ājagāma pituḥ veśma 
sāvitrī saha mantribhiḥ ॥

*अन्वयः- **anvayaḥ-*

ततः सावित्री, सह मन्त्रिभिः, सर्वाणि एव तीर्थानि तथा आश्रमान् अभिगम्य, पितुः 
वेश्म आजगाम ॥

tataḥ sāvitrī, saha mantribhiḥ, sarvāṇi eva tīrthāni tathā āśramān 
abhigamya, pituḥ veśma ājagāma ॥

*प्रतिपदार्थः- **pratipadārthaḥ-*

ततः = tataḥ = then

सावित्री = sāvitrī = sāvitrī

सह मन्त्रिभिः = saha mantribhiḥ = along with ministers

सर्वाणि एव = sarvāṇi eva = all

तीर्थानि = tīrthāni = holy places, sacred waters

तथा = tathā = and similarly

आश्रमान् = āśramān = hermitages

अभिगम्य = abhigamya = having visited

पितुः = pituḥ = of father

वेश्म = veśma = home

आजगाम = ājagāma = came back

*तात्पर्यम्- **tātparyam-*

1. Then, about the same time Savitri, after visiting all the holy places and 
the cloistered ashramas, returned along with the ministers back to her 
father’s house. [2]

2. Savitri, accompanied by the king’s counsellors, came to her father’s 
abode after having visited various sacred regions and asylums.

Verse - 3.278.4

*मूलम्- **mūlam-*

नारद उवाच॥ nārada uvāca॥

क्व गताभूत्सुतेयं ते कुतश्चैवागता नृप । 

किमर्थं युवतीं भर्त्रे न चैनां सम्प्रयच्छसि ॥4॥

kva gatābhūtsuteyaṃ te kutaścaivāgatā nṛpa । 

kimarthaṃ yuvatīṃ bhartre na caināṃ samprayacchasi ॥4॥

*पदविभागः- **padavibhāgaḥ-*

क्व गता अभूत् सुता इयं ते कुतः च एव आगता नृप । किमर्थं युवतीं भर्त्रे न च 
एनां सम्प्रयच्छसि ॥

kva gatā abhūt sutā iyaṃ te kutaḥ ca eva āgatā nṛpa । kimarthaṃ yuvatīṃ 
bhartre na ca enāṃ samprayacchasi ॥

*अन्वयः- **anvayaḥ-*

नृप, ते सुता इयं क्व गता अभूत् ? कुतः चैव आगता ? किमर्थं एनां युवतीं 
भर्त्रे न च सम्प्रयच्छसि ? ॥4॥

nṛpa, te sutā iyaṃ kva gatā abhūt ? kutaḥ caiva āgatā ? kimarthaṃ enāṃ 
yuvatīṃ bhartre na ca samprayacchasi ?॥

*प्रतिपदार्थः- **pratipadārthaḥ-*

नृप = nṛpa = O king

ते = te = your

सुता = sutā = daughter

इयं = iyaṃ = she 

क्व = kva = where

गता अभूत् = gatā abhūt = went

कुतः च एव = kutaḥ ca eva = from where

आगता = āgatā = has come back

किमर्थं = kimarthaṃ = why

एनां = enāṃ = her

युवतीं = yuvatīṃ = young lady (obj.)

भर्त्रे = bhartre = to husband (lit. for)

न च सम्प्रयच्छसि = na ca samprayacchasi = not give (in marriage)

*तात्पर्यम्- **tātparyam-*

1. Narad said– On what mission, O King, did your daughter go and wherefrom 
is she returning? and why, now that she is a good young woman, do you not 
give her in marriage to a suitable husband? [4]

2. And Narada then said, ‘Whither had this thy daughter gone ? And, king, 
whence also doth she come ? Why also dost thou not bestow her on a husband, 
now that she hath arrived at the age of puberty ?’

अनेन एव कार्येण खलु प्रेषिता, अद्य एव आगता च । देवर्षे, तद् शृणु अस्याः 
भर्तारं, यः अनया वृतः ॥5॥

anena eva kāryeṇa khalu preṣitā, adya eva āgatā ca । devarṣe, tad śṛṇu asyāḥ 
bhartāraṃ, yaḥ anayā vṛtaḥ ॥

*प्रतिपदार्थः- **pratipadārthaḥ-*

अनेन एव = anena eva = this alone (lit. by this)

कार्येण = kāryeṇa = on work (lit. by)

खलु = khalu = indeed

प्रेषिता = preṣitā = sent

अद्य एव च = adya eva ca = today only

आगता = āgatā = came, returned

देवर्षे = devarṣe = O sage of gods

तद् = tad = that

शृणु = śṛṇu = listen

अस्याः = asyāḥ = her

भर्तारं = bhartāraṃ = husband (obj.)

यः = yaḥ = that who

अनया = anayā = by her

वृतः = vṛtaḥ = chosen, selected

*तात्पर्यम्- **tātparyam-*

1. Aswapati said– It was indeed with this intent, O God-sage, that I sent 
her in quest and it is after that she has just returned; let us hear from 
her of the one whom she must have chosen for her lord and husband. [5]

2. Aswapati answered, saying, ‘Surely it was on this very business that she 
had been sent, and she returneth now (from her search). Do thou, O celestial 
sage, listen, even from her as to the husband she hath chosen herself !’
