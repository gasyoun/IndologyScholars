## 7. ShaSa — 2015-07-15 13:04:39

Namaste

पदविभागः - पदानां विभागः। विभागः is to separate things-  here, words. 

The words in the shloka are in sandhi and samAsa, so it needs a breaking of 

those words to understand them separately.

It is traditional way of learning to first identify the words distinctly, 

   and then spot out the order according to one's language, 

   arrange the words in prose-order (anvaya) and then translate to 

understand the whole shloka. 

When sandhi is in same word, it should not be broken. 

My way is to separate samAsa words with a hyphen, but tradition does not 

always permit this.

Hope this helps.

One clarification-

pada vibhAga is one word. So pada-vibhAga is correct (with a hyphen). My 

mistake to give them separate..
