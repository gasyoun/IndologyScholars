## 2. Radim Navyan — 2015-02-01 19:26:15

I'm not that kind. So here's the answer:



तत् कर्म यत् न बन्धाय, सा विद्या या विमुक्तये।

आयासाय अपरं कर्म, विद्या अन्या शिल्प–नौपुणम्॥



The translation is:

Карма - то, что не спутывает, видья та, что выпутывает.

Прочая карма - чтоб тусоваться, прочая видья -  умелость артиста.



Nothing but a brave slogan.

Anyway you'll need more effort to understand the contextual meaning of 

karma and vidya. You couldn't find such karma and vidya in nowaday's life.

## 5. Radim Navyan — 2015-02-01 21:35:50

There's no "standard" for Devanagari writing.



I've used sandhi-free type of writing, making your reading more comfort,

whereas linked text is a "gentelman's agreement" now-a-day. In 

scriptures the latter one is customary, not mine.
