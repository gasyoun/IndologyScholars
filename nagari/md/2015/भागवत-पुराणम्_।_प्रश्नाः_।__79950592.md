## 1. Mandala Pati (Владислав) — 2015-06-10 17:20:18

*In this topic I want to put questions based on the grammar of 

भागवत-पुराणम्.*



 

In 10.43.6 <http://www.vedabase.com/sb/10/43/6> we have samasa 

*karīndras (karin + indraḥ). By which rule we delete "n" in "karin" while 

making samasa?*

 





*karīndras tam abhidrutya kareṇa tarasāgrahītkarād vigalitaḥ so ’muṁ 

nihatyāṅghriṣv alīyata*

   

*kari <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=kari>* — of 

elephants; *indraḥ <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=indrah>* 

— the lord; *tam <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=tam>* — 

Him; *abhidrutya 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=abhidrutya>* — running 

toward; *kareṇa <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=karena>* — 

with his trunk; *tarasā 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=tarasa>* — violently; *agrahīt 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=agrahit>* — seized; *karāt 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=karat>* — from the trunk; *vigalitaḥ 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=vigalitah>* — slipping 

away; *saḥ <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=sah>* — He, 

Kṛṣṇa; *amum <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=amum>* — him, 

Kuvalayāpīḍa; *nihatya 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=nihatya>* — striking; *aṅghriṣu 

<http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=anghrisu>* — among his 

legs; *alīyata <http://vedabase.com/en/synonyms-index?original=aliyata>* — 

He disappeared.

 

The lord of the elephants charged Kṛṣṇa and violently seized Him with his 

trunk. But Kṛṣṇa slipped away, struck him a blow and disappeared from his 

view among his legs.

## 4. narayan prasad — 2015-06-10 19:20:02

<< By which rule or paribhāṣā we condiser "karin" in samasa as separate

pada?>>



P.2.4.71 सुपो धातु-प्रातिपदिकयोः ।

will cause deletion of the case suffix सु (= स्) and so you don't see this

suffix.



P.1.1.62 प्रत्ययलोपे प्रत्ययलक्षणम् ।

P.1.4.14 सुप्तिङन्तं पदम् ।
