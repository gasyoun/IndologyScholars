## 2. Kartik K — 2015-02-10 11:28:46

Dear Tori,

Literal translation of "My family is my rock" would be शैलेव मे कुलम्
however since this sentence is not how it would be normally used in
Sanskrit parlance, it would be better to translate it is "My family is my
rock hard foundation." Which would be: शैलपोटेव मे कुलम्.

The translations provided above are done bearing in mind the least number
of letters possible because you want to make it as a tattoo.

Regards,
Kartik

## 5. Kartik K — 2015-02-10 15:59:10

Dear Vladimir,

The words used in the sentence are as follows:

   1. शैलः = rock (masculine, nominative singular)
   2. पोटः = foundation (masculine, nominative singular)
   3. इव​ = like, similar to (indeclinable)
   4. मे = my (personal pronoun, dative or genitive singular)
   5. कुलम् = family (neuter, nominative singular)

The word शैलपोटः is defined as: "शैलस्य पोटः" इति "शैलपोटः"
षष्ठीततपुरुषसमासोऽयम् = शैलपोटः is a genitive compound which means
'rock-solid foundation'.

*Etymology of the masculine word पोटः is as follows:*
The word पोटः is derived form the root पुट् संश्लेषे तुदादिः - पुट् is a
तुदादि root which is used in the sense of 'connecting or bonding'. In this
sense the word पोटः means 'foundation of a house' that binds or connects
the house to the earth.

*Etymology of the feminine word पोटा is as follows:*
The word पोटा is derived from the root पुट् संसर्गे चुरादिः - पुट् is a
चुरादि root which is used in the sense of 'union or mixing'. In this sense
the word पोटा means 'hermaphrodite' is because a hermaphrodite is of
'mixed' gender possessing mixed male and female characteristics.

वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम्  = वसुधा एव कुटुम्बकम् which means "The entire earth
is indeed a family". That is not what Tori wanted translated.

Hence, *शैलपोट इव मे कुलम्* which literally means "My family is like a
rock-solid foundation for me" is the closest to the English phrase "My
family is my rock."

This is my humble opinion. Let me know your thoughts.

Yours,
Kartik chandra Shastri

>>>The words used in the sentence are as follows:
>>>*  शैलः = rock (masculine, nominative singular)
>>>*  पोटः = foundation (masculine, nominative singular)
>>>*  इव​ = like, similar to (indeclinable)
>>>*  मे = my (personal pronoun, dative or genitive singular)
>>>*  कुलम् = family (neuter, nominative singular)
>>>The word शैलपोटः is defined as: "शैलस्य पोटः" इति "शैलपोटः" षष्ठीततपुरुषसमासोऽयम् = शैलपोटः is a genitive compound which means 'rock-solid foundation'.
>>> 
>>>Etymology of the masculine word पोटः is as follows:
>>>The word पोटः is derived form the root पुट् संश्लेषे तुदादिः - पुट् is a तुदादि root which is used in the sense of 'connecting or bonding'. In this sense the word पोटः means 'foundation of a house' that binds or connects the house to the earth.
>>>
>>>Etymology of the feminine word पोटा is as follows:
>>>The word पोटा is derived from the root पुट् संसर्गे चुरादिः - पुट् is a चुरादि root which is used in the sense of 'union or mixing'. In this sense the word पोटा means 'hermaphrodite' is because a hermaphrodite is of 'mixed' gender possessing mixed male and female characteristics.
>>>

There is nothing metaphorical about धरुणं - it just means support or
foundation. Metaphor is only when you compare two objects. It is a figure
of speech.

Мета́фора — слово или выражение, употребляемое в переносном значении, в
основе которого лежит неназванное сравнение предмета с каким-либо другим на
основании их общего признака.
