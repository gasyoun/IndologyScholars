## 2. Kartik K — 2015-07-12 00:16:22

मा = do not (Indeclinable)

न = not (Indeclinable)

भूवं = I will/shall be (First person singular Injunctive)

भूयासम् = May I be *OR* Let me be (First person singular Benedictive)



*TRANSLATION: *

May I never not be.

*OR*

Let me never not be.



In simple words this is the quality of the soul which is eternal by nature.



Every living entity thinks: "Let me be forever. Let me never not be".



Hope this helps.

## 6. narayan prasad — 2015-07-12 15:19:10

In Sanskrit, the order of words may be kept same as in Russian as follows:

Я        не    потому       не        пойду           на поминки,

что      была       эта        неприятность.

अहम्    न      तस्मात्        न         गमिष्यामि           मृतकभोजाय,

       यत्     आसीत्      एतत्        अप्रियत्वम् ।



But in any modern Indo-Aryan languages, the word equivalent to "потому"

(तस्मात्) will come earlier to "न", so that the two "не" (न) occur side by

side.
