## 2. Narayan Prasad — 2019-11-11 09:56:55

<<Prerequisites:
(1)   You must know at least one Indian language properly.>>

In the link given in my earlier message, this condition will be hardly 
applicable for Russians. 
So in such cases, I would suggest that they start from scratch (without 
bothering about Panini grammar) with the following graded books for basic 
Sanskrit texts: 
    (1) Samskrita Baladarsa 
    (2) Samskrita Prathamadarsa 
    (3) Samskrita Dvitiyadarsa 
    (4) Samskrita Tritiyadarsa.

After this they could start Panini, but side by side reading some Sanskrit 
literature as per their interest.

Best wishes
Narayan Prasad
