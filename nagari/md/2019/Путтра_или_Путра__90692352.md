*पुत्त्र*  m. (-त्त्रः) 1 A son. 2 A child, in the language of the  Ve'das. 

3 The fifth mansion from the point of conjunction of the sun, and a 

zodiacal sign. f. (-त्त्री) A daughter. du. always, (त्त्रौ) Son and 

daughter. E. पुत्, and त्रा to preserve, the hell, (from,) aff. क; or पूञ् 

to purify, aff. त्त्र; one त *may be rejected in writing this word, and its 

derivatives, leaving **पुत्र**.*
