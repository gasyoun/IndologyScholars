## 3. Narayan Prasad — 2019-12-29 19:07:04

Здравствуйте!
सत् (obtained from the धातु अस् with the suffix शतृ) can be declined like 
धावत् or गच्छत् in all the three genders.
Best wishes
Narayan Prasad
