## 2. Usha Sanka — 2021-10-25 13:40:34

Namaste

This is the meaning actually-

सुमुखः , पुं, (शोभनं मुखं यस्य ।)

sumukhaḥ , puṃ, (śobhanaṃ mukhaṃ yasya ।)

Direct meaning- One who has "good face" 

or 

Implied meaning - "one whose face is auspicious to look at"
