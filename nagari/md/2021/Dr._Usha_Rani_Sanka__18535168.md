## 1. Marcis — 2021-12-08 18:06:46

Dr. Usha Rani Sanka has done her PhD in Vyakarana, after Acharya 
(Vyakarana) and MA (Sanskrit literature).
She has over 15 years of experience in teaching Sanskrit at various levels, 
both as a lecturer in college and private virtual classes. 
Proficient in Sanskrit, Telugu, Hindi, and English, she writes and teaches 
extensively in all 4 languages.
She possesses a rich experience of proofreading, editing, and DTP of 
Sanskrit works and translations.
