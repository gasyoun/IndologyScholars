# Всё про хаван-кунд и хому. Библиография

[Читать оригинальный тред в Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/d/msgid/nagari/ee552d87-a018-48c3-9c68-2d30c756bb13n@googlegroups.com)

## 1. JC — 2021-08-01 13:34:19

Нашел интересную объёмистую библиографию касательно установлений о 

жертвеннике. В ней есть интересные источники.



(С хинди акцентом на английском)





   1. Science and Technology in Medieval India – A Bibliography (A. Raman), 

   2. Agni Puran Chapter 24 (edited by Baldev Upadhyay, Chowkambha, 

   Varanasi), 

   3. Ajit Agama which is the 5th Agama of the 10 Saiva Agama (Published by 

   France Institute of Indology, Pondicherry, 1964), 

   4. Chaturvarg Chintamani of Hemadri (13th Century A.D.). Kunds are 

   treated in Dankhand (of this encyclopedic work) which is printed as Vol. 1 

   by Asiatic Soc., Calcutta, 1971. 

   5. Grihyaagnisar (1640) of Narayan Bhatt, Kund portion is found in 

   manuscript (no. 8613) at Oriental Instution, Baroda. 

   6. Karmakand Karmavali (1073 A.D.) of Somsambhu, Published in Kashmir 

   Series of Texts and Studies, No. 73, 1947, 

   7. Kund-camatkara of Trayambak Bhairav, Ms. no. 1300 in Oriantal 

   Institute, Baroda, 

   8. Kundchitra, Bhanuvidha, Indian Museum, Calcutta, Ms. 2925 A., 

   9. Kund-darpan (1724) of Mallesvara Aradhye, CESS (A), V, P. 286 for 

   manuscript and extract, 

   10. Kund-dipa of Maji with autocommentary (1600 A.D.) CESS (A), V, P. 

   269 for manuscripts and extracts, 

   11. Kund-ganit with commentry (anonymous), cf. ms. No. 203 in Bhandakar 

   Oriental Research Institute, Pune, (NCC, Vol. 4, P. 178), 

   12. "Kundgrant-vimshati (=KGV) A collection of 20 Kund text published by 

   H.  Venkatraman, Aryasevak Printer, Mumbai, 1887. Works included in KGV are 

   Kund Siddhi, Kundark, Kund Mandap Darpan, Kund Martand, Kund Kumudi, Kund 

   Karika, Kund Sulab Karika, Kundpradip, Kundodadhi, Kund Ratnakar, 

   Kundarnava, 

   13. Kundodyota, Kundprakaran, Kund Tatva Pradip, Kund Kalpadrum, Kund 

   Rachna, Kund Mandap Nirnay Lakshan, Kund Akruti, Kund Maricimala," 

   14. Kund Kadambari (1741) of Gokulanatha, ed. by D. Jha and K. Jha, 

   Sanskrit Univ., Darbhanga, 1982. 

   15. Kundkalpadrum (1655) of Madhava Shukla (who also Wrote a commentary 

   on it). It is printed as 16th work in KGV. 

   16. Kund-kalplata of Dhundhiraja Saiva (16th cent) see CESS (A), V, 126 

   for manuscripts and extract. 

   17. Kund-Karika of Laksmidhara. It is 6th work in KGV. 

   18. Kundakruti (1449) of Ramacandra Vajapeyi. It is an early and well 

   known work on the subject. Author himself wrote two commentaries on it. 

   Printed as 19th work in KGV. 

   19. Kund-lakshan as the 25th parisista of Atharv-ved see NCC, IV, 185 

   for manuscriptsand printed version. 

   20. Kundloka is the name of author’s own commentary on 

   Kund-mandap-kaumudi (q.v.). 

   21. Kunda-mandana of Gokulanatha frequently quoted by him in his 

   Kund-kadambari. 

   22. Kund-mandana of Narhari Saptarshi with auto-commentary. See CESS 

   (A), V, 164-165 for mss. and abstract. 

   23. Kund-mandap-darpan (1578) of Narayan (son of Ananta). It is 3rd work 

   in KGV. 

   24. Kund-mandapadi-lakshan, an anonymous compendium of quotations. Ms. 

   no. 4616 (a) at Oriental Inst., Baroda. 

   25. Kund-mandap-kaumudi (16th cent.) of Visvanath Deva. It is the 5th 

   work in KGV. 

   26. Kund-mandap-nirnay-laksan from Rudra-paddhati or Parasurdma—paddhati 

   attributed to Parashuram. It is the 18th work in KGV. 

   27. Kund—mandap-vidhi of Bodhayana. Ms. No. 10440 in Prajna Pathasala, 

   Wai, Satara. See NCC, vol. XIV (2001), p. 104. 

   28. Kund-marici-mala of Visnu as 20th work in KGV. 

   29. Kunda-martand (1691) of Govinda, son of Gadadhara It is the 4th work 

   in KGV. 

   30. Kund-martand by Shankara. Ms. No. 223 in Sanskrit School, Rajapur, 

   Ratnagiri. See NCC, IV, p. 184. 

   31. Kund-nidhi (or Kund-vidhi) of Vishveswara, son of Lakshman. Ms. 9511 

   at Oriental Inst., Baroda. 

   32. Kundanuksa of Gangadharanand, printed as the 12th work in KGV. 

   33. Kund-paddhati of Nagoji Bhatta (c. 18th cent.) see CESS (A), V, 168 

   etc. for more on the author. 

   34. Kund-prabandh (1632) of Kalidasa, son of Balbhadra. See NCC, IV, p. 

   180 for more information. 

   35. Kund-pradip (1639) of Mahadeva who also wrote a commentary on it. 

   Printed as the 8th work in KGV. 

   36. Kund-prakaran from the Narad-prakran (a well-known work). It is 

   printed as 14th text in KGV. 

   37. Kund-praksha (c. 1700) of Rudradeva Toro. See CESS (A), V, pp. 

   505-506 for more on the author and the work. 

   38. Kund-rachna (sutra and commentary). It is the 17th work in KGV. 

   39. Kund-ratnakara (16th century?) of Vishvanath Dwivedi (who also wrote 

   a commentary on it). It is the 10th work in KGV. Its earliest manuscript is 

   dated 1588. 

   40. Kund—ratnavali (1868) of Ramcandra Dikshit (alias Jade), with 

   commentary Manjusha, published by Nimaya Sagar Press, Bombay, 1912. 

   41. Kundark (17th century) of Shankara Bhatt, son of Nilakantha. It is 

   the 2nd work in KGV. 

   42. Kundarnava os Sridhara Agnihotri, son of Surjia. It is the 11th work 

   in KGV. 

   43. Kundsar of Vinayak Pandurang Khanapurakara (born 1858). 

   44. Kund-siddhyudhrti of Ganesh, son of Sridhar. It is a prose 

   commentary on a portion of Mandap-kund siddhi of Vitthala, ed. by Takao 

   Hayashi in Bulletin of the National Museum of Ethnology, Osaka, 12(1) 

   (1987), 199-. 

   45. Kund-siromani (1599) of Vishram, son of Purusottam See CESS (A) V, 

   658-659 formanuscripts and extract. 

   46. Kund-sulba-karika as 7th work in KGV. 

   47. Kund-tattva-pradip (1623) of Balbhadra Sukla (who also wrote a tika 

   later on). It is 15th work in KGV. 

   48. Kund-vibhushan of Ram Dev. See CESS (A), V, 426. 

   49. Kundodadhi of Ramchandra as 9th work in KGV. 

   50. Kundoddyota (17th cent.) of Nilkanth Bhatt as the 13th work in KGV. 

   His son Shankara wrote two commentaries on it. 

   51. Mandap-drum (1654) of Mahadev Suri, son of Manilava, Ed. By 

   Sreekrishna Sharma in Adyar Library Bulletin Vol. 22, parts 1-2 (1958), 

   119-157. 

   52. Mandap-kund-siddhi (1620) of Vitthal Dikshit. It is a standard and 

   popular work and is 1st item in KGV. There is an auto-commentary.  See CESS 

   (A), V, 638-643 for details. 

   53. Shardatilak (15th cent. or earlier) of Laksamanadesi Kendra.  

   Raghavabhatta’s commentary on it is dated 1493. 

   54. Siddhant-tattva-vivek (1658) of Kamalakar with auto-commentary. 

   Agni-kunda are dealt with in the Chapter III. See Ganit-Bharati, vol. 20 

   (1998), 8-24, for a study. 

   55. Vhacaspatyam, a Sanskrit encyclopedia, Vol. III (reprinted, 

   Varanasi, 1990), deals with kunda (pp. 2076-2087). 

   56. Vedi-kund-nirnay (c. 1600) of Vishvanath, son of Anant. Ms. No. 1759 

   in Anup Sanskrit Library, Bikaner.
