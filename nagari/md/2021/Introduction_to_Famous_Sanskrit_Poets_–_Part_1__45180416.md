We are delighted to announce a New Free Live Webinar Course
 "प्रसिद्धसंस्कृतकवीनां परिचयः - भागः १"
"Introduction to Famous Sanskrit Poets – Part 1"

The course deals with ten great poets of Sanskrit Literature starting from 
Valmiki. This course also covers a part of the syllabus of the Chittoor 
Samartha Examination.

Discover the poetic genius of these poets through an in-depth
analysis of the characteristic features of their poetry.

Helpful structure and tips for presenting detailed answers about the
poets in any exam.

Sanskrit
