Здравствуйте,



посмотрите плагин к браузеру Хром -



описание - http://sa.diglossa.org/



Установить можно из chrome webstore -



https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/morpheus-chromium-plugin/bochopmkmeclhfljekbigjdedacgkanc



краткое описание:





three steps:



double click everywhere - padaccheda - possible chains of padas; the upper

- the better



click on pada - morphplogic analysis (names analysis more or less correct,

verbs in its infancy)



click on morph row - possible dicts articles; note: you should choose a

corresponding gender or gana-pada by yourself , i.e. check morphs to dicts

correspondance



ESC - closes all



shift-mouse-over - IAST transliteration



editor - just type devanagari, keyboard layout mimics SLP1, then Enter or

doubleclick



detailed description: http://sa.diglossa.org/
