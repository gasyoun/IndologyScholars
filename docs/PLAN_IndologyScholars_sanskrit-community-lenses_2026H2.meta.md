# Metadoc — PLAN_IndologyScholars_sanskrit-community-lenses_2026H2.md

This is the companion record for the
[execution plan](https://github.com/gasyoun/IndologyScholars/blob/main/docs/PLAN_IndologyScholars_sanskrit-community-lenses_2026H2.md).
It records why the plan exists, how it was produced, what may improve it, and
how it should be maintained. It does not duplicate the plan.

## Subject

- **Purpose:** prepare an execution-ready comparison layer and Russian article
  revision centred on the Roerich and Zograf conference corpus.
- **Audience:** the executing Codex task, the article author, and later
  maintainers of the five-lens comparison package.
- **Contract:** dated plain Markdown; full blob URLs; 25 interview rulings;
  five cross-linked planning layers; explicit autonomy and evidence limits.
- **Status:** active, local, uncommitted, and not published.

## Provenance

- Created 29-07-2026 through `/ask`, after a four-round, 25-ruling interview.
- Planning route: best-effort root planning explicitly authorized after the
  configured Fable planner was unavailable.
- Model: Codex, GPT-5 family; the runtime did not expose a more specific root
  model identifier.
- Audit inputs: IndologyScholars, nagari, ORS/VK, the canonical
  IndologyArchiveAtlas feed contract, the public BVP Google Group, cross-repo
  hubs, current issues/PRs, and official ACL Anthology documentation.
- No H-number was minted because the only approved minting tool pushes to
  `origin/main`, while the user's fence explicitly forbids pushing.

## Known limitations

- The BVP archive is public and large, but complete listing enumeration and
  pagination have not yet been demonstrated. Raw captures remain local; every
  derivative carries measured enumeration/fetch/parse coverage.
- Source counts in the plan are audit observations from different snapshots,
  not a synchronized release.
- Geographic labels describe forum orientation and observed affiliation, not
  nationality, ethnicity, or exhaustive civilizational membership.
- Exact quotations remain subject to context, source stability, and removal of
  unrelated contact details.
- The plan is locally authored in an isolated worktree and has no public blob
  until the user later authorizes delivery.

## Intended use and known misuse

Use this plan to build a comparison package that keeps each medium's native
unit and denominator. Do not use it to concatenate messages, posts, and talks
into a single count; to claim a census of Sanskrit scholarship; to infer
identity from name similarity; or to treat expert corpus-selection judgments
as population statistics.

## Maintenance and sunset

IndologyScholars owns the comparison layer. IndologyArchiveAtlas remains the
canonical owner of INDOLOGY-L. This plan sunsets when Wave 1 passes and the
Russian article revision has its own reproducible snapshot and verification
record. It is superseded only by a later linked plan that preserves the 25
rulings or explicitly records their revision.
